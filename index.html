The 3D Corridor: The Entrance to This Portfolio

An Interactive Portfolio Instead of a Static Page

This site is the interactive portfolio of wu5, an AI engineer and creative developer working on AI engineering, agent systems and research tooling. Instead of a flat list of links, the entrance is a hand-drawn 3D corridor rendered in the browser with React Three Fiber, Three.js and GSAP. Every texture and transition was authored for this project.

From the corridor, four doors lead to the four rooms of the portfolio: the gallery of open-source projects, the about room with skills and milestones, the studio with repositories and notes, and the contact room with social signposts. Each room is reachable directly by URL, so no part of the portfolio is hidden behind the 3D scene.

About wu5: AI Engineer / Creative Developer

What wu5 Works On

wu5 builds AI engineering systems: agent architectures, task routing and governance, plus the research tooling and pipelines that keep experiments reproducible. The work in this portfolio spans auditable spec-first development workflows, agent-skill collections and neural surrogates for engineering simulation.

Skills Shown in the About Room

The skills are displayed as floating balloons in the 3D about room:

  • React and Next.js for the interface layer of 3D applications.
  • Three.js and React Three Fiber for WebGL scenes, shaders and interactive rooms.
  • GSAP for the camera choreography and room transitions.
  • JavaScript, HTML and CSS for the underlying interaction and layout.
  • Git for the workflow discipline behind every published repository.
  • Figma for the hand-drawn art direction and Firebase for supporting services.
